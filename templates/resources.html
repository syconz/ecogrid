Energy Saving Resources

Simple facts and habits that make a real difference to your energy bill and carbon footprint.

💡

Switch to LED Bulbs

LED bulbs use up to 80% less energy than incandescent bulbs and last 25x longer.

🌡️

Smart Thermostat Use

Lowering your thermostat by just 1°C can cut heating costs by roughly 10% annually.

🔌

Unplug Idle Devices

Devices in standby mode ("phantom load") can account for up to 10% of household electricity use.

☀️

Use Off-Peak Hours

Running high-energy appliances during solar-optimal hours (see Renewable Advisor) reduces grid strain and cost.

🌍

1 kWh Saved = Less CO2

On average, saving 1 kWh of grid electricity prevents roughly 0.4–0.9 kg of CO2 emissions, depending on your region's energy mix.

🏠

Seal Air Leaks

Gaps around doors and windows can waste 10-15% of heating/cooling energy — a $5 sealant fixes most of it.
